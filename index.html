01 / THE IDEA

Access should be
continuous.

A ramp at one entrance is not enough if the path ends at a kerb, a step, or a locked lift. AccessPath is a concept for making the whole journey easier to understand and improve.

Know what each route symbol means.

↗RampA ramp is listed in the route notes. Its condition and continuity must be checked on site.
⇧LiftA lift is listed in the route notes. Availability and operating status must be confirmed.
⌁StairsSteps or stairs may prevent a step-free journey for some users.

These indicators explain the prototype. They do not certify any LPU building or route as accessible.
